# Public dataset catalogue

The machine-readable registry is `datasets/registry.yml`. This document records the intended scientific role of each source.

## Core energy evidence

- **InSecTT WSN Power Consumption Dataset**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.7762712`: BLE and Thread high-resolution power traces, plus UWB and EPhESOS.
- **NB-IoT vs. LTE-M energy measurements**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.7603641`: raw measurements and phase-level analysis for NB-IoT/LTE-M and HTTP/MQTT.
- **LoRaWAN LR-FHSS current traces**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.13838241`: ACK/no-ACK and data-rate variants.
- **NB-IoT Power Measurements**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.3557239`: supplementary historical validation.

## Coverage and reliability evidence

- **LoED**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.4121430`: urban LoRaWAN gateway metadata.
- **Rome 4G/NB-IoT/5G dataset**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.8224890`: operational coverage and performance.
- **Rome/Oslo NB-IoT coverage**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.7674299`.
- **NB-IoT and 5G coverage companion dataset**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.14867061`.
- **LoRa signal quality and GPS**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.13835721`.
- **LoRaWAN harbour RSSI dataset**, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.10142174`.

## Optional Kaggle evidence

- Position-Annotated BLE RSSI Dataset, CC BY 4.0.
- BLE RSSI Dataset for Indoor Localization, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Kaggle datasets support environment and mobility modelling only; they do not supply protocol-energy measurements.

# Two-paper publication plan

## Paper A — STACKWISE Empirical Evidence Benchmark

Working title: **STACKWISE Empirical Evidence Benchmark: A Harmonised Multi-Source Dataset for Layer-Aware IoT Communication Stack Analysis**

Core sections:
1. Background and motivation for a harmonised empirical benchmark.
2. Source datasets, provenance, licences and acquisition.
3. Harmonisation methodology and canonical data model.
4. Measurement boundaries, statistical units, dependence and uncertainty metadata.
5. Benchmark scenario/stack definitions and packaged layers.
6. Technical validation, QA, reproducibility, data availability and reuse notes.

Do not include hypothesis-testing results from Experiments 1–5.

## Paper B — STACKWISE methodology/results

Working title: **STACKWISE: Feasibility-, Evidence- and Uncertainty-Aware Selection of IoT Communication Stacks**

Core sections:
1. Introduction and decision problem.
2. Related work and gap.
3. STACKWISE framework: layers, admissibility, feasibility, uncertainty, accounting and fleet portfolios.
4. Frozen Benchmark v1.0.0 input (brief summary only).
5. Experiment design.
6. Results: Experiments 1–5.
7. Discussion: why feasibility, admissibility, dependence and accounting boundaries change conclusions.
8. Limitations: no global stochastic ranking; no matched whole-device cellular energy.
9. Conclusion.

Main-paper figure budget: six figures. Main-table budget: three tables. Move technical matrices, full grids and sensitivity tables to supplementary material.
